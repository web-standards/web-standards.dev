The WebKit team presents a 27.3 beta feature that shows light websites as dark when the system is in dark mode. Sites with `color-scheme: light dark` are left alone. Others get luminosity inverted with hue preserved, text contrast kept at 4.5:1, and simple logos and SVG icons adjusted. To opt out, use `color-scheme: light only`. Better yet, build real dark mode with `prefers-color-scheme` and `light-dark()`.
