The `<install>` element and `navigator.install()` let pages offer to install a web app, even cross-origin. Media pseudo-classes like `:playing`, `:paused`, and `:buffering` style `<audio>` and `<video>` by playback state. The `::highlight(*)` selector styles all custom highlights at once, `flow-start` and `flow-end` join the alignment keywords, and the `side` attribute on `<textPath>` moves text to the other side of a path. WebHID comes to Android.
