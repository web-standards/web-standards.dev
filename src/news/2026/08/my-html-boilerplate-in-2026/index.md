Manuel Matuzović walks through every line of his `<head>` and explains why it’s there: charset before the title, viewport, canonical, Open Graph, and a print stylesheet. New this year are the `text-scale` meta tag for OS text size on mobile, an SVG favicon that adapts to dark mode, a markdown alternate link for LLMs, and `fediverse:creator` for Mastodon attribution. Scripts default to `type="module"`.
