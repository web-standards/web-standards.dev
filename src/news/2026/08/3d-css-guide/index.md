Carmen Ansio walks through building real 3D scenes in CSS, framing it as the leap from Super Mario Bros 3’s painted depth to Super Mario 64’s true camera. The tour covers `perspective` and `perspective-origin` to place a camera, `preserve-3d` to keep real depth, reusable cuboids, animated camera moves, and `translateZ`, `rotateX/Y/Z`, `scale3d`, and `matrix3d`. All six properties are Baseline.
