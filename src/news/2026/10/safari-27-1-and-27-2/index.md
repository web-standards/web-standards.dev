Jen Simmons covers two releases. Safari 27.1 brings Device Posture and Viewport Segments APIs for iPhone Duo behind flags, and two-page PDFs when folded. Safari 27.2 adds `::field-content` for customizable `<select>` and renames `flow-tolerance` to `fit-tolerance` in Grid Lanes. With `align-self: safe center`, absolutely positioned boxes stay inside their container. Plus 20 bug fixes.
