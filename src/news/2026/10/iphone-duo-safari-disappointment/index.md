Mike Piontek tested his sites on the iPhone Duo simulator and found that Safari doesn’t treat its right-hand toolbar as a safe area, so `env()` insets don’t help. Instead, Safari tints it by sampling page colors 5 to 10 pixels from the edge, an undocumented behavior that ignores `theme-color` and anything not `fixed` or `sticky`. His workaround for other devices, a fixed strip along the top, now looks cut off.
