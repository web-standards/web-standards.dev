Brecht De Ruyte shows how `random()` takes a minimum, a maximum, and an optional step in matching units, so letters tilt, cards wobble, and marquee rows move at different speeds without JavaScript. Shared keys keep related properties in sync, and `element-scoped` makes values unique per element. It’s already in Safari, Chrome, and Firefox behind a flag, so wrap it in `@supports` and use it for decorative effects.
