Darius Cepulis found two Safari feature flags in the iPhone Duo simulator: the Device Posture API, which reports whether the screen is `continuous` or `folded`, and the Viewport Segments API, which exposes both halves via media queries and `env()` variables. He used them to build a Netflix-style player with video on top and controls below. On a Pixel Fold emulator, fullscreen fills only the top half.
