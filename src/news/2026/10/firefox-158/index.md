JPEG XL is now enabled by default, with lossy and lossless compression, transparency, animation, and HDR. CSS typed arithmetic lets `calc()` divide values with different units, like `100vw / 1px`, to get a unitless number. Notifications gain a `navigate` option with a URL to open on click, and WebTransport adds `getStats()`. Behind flags: the `random()` function, `corner-shape`, and `import defer`.
